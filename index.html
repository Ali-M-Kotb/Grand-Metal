Why Duplex 2205

Built for corrosive, high-pressure environments

Duplex 2205 combines an austenitic-ferritic microstructure that delivers roughly double the yield strength of standard austenitic stainless steels, along with excellent resistance to chloride stress-corrosion cracking and pitting — the standard choice for RO seawater desalination, marine and offshore piping.

High Corrosion Resistance

Superior resistance to chloride pitting and stress-corrosion cracking versus 304/316 grades.

High Mechanical Strength

Roughly double the yield strength of austenitic stainless, enabling thinner, lighter piping.

Desalination Ready

Specified for RO seawater desalination and MSF plants exposed to concentrated brine.

Certified Standards

Manufactured to ASTM A815 (fittings) and industry SCH 40 dimensional standards.
